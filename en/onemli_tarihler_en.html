11th Türkiye Viticulture and Technology Symposium with International Participation

Theme: Sustainable Viticulture in the Context of Climate Change and Local Values

11th Türkiye Viticulture and Technology Symposium with International Participation is organised to foster information exchange by gathering together recent research, innovative practices and demands of the viticulture sector, and to promote collaboration between researchers and stakeholders. Particular emphasis will be placed on local grape varieties, the genetic resources, and the potential for their future utilization in viticulture.
